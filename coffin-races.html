13TH ANNUAL DOWNTOWN TONOPAH SPOOK FESTIVAL & HAUNT

COFFIN
RACES

SATURDAY · OCTOBER 24 · 4 PM

Start creating your team and begin building and decorating your coffins. $25 registration fee per team.

Race Rules

  • At least 5 team members: 1 rider and at least 4 pallbearers.
  • Pallbearers must wear flat-heeled, closed-toe shoes.
  • Rider must be over 5 years old, wear safety headgear, and remain seated or lying down.
  • Human-powered only. Coffins must be carried—no lift assist, wheels, motors, engines or mechanical propulsion.
  • Cardboard is acceptable if sturdy. No sharp edges or exposed nails or screws.
  • Minimum depth: 15 inches. Maximum width: 3 feet. Minimum length: 6 feet.
  • Decorations may reach 10 feet but must be removable if they obstruct safety.
  • Build, costuming and decorations are part of judging and rewards.
  • At least 4 pallbearers must remain in direct contact with the coffin during the race or the team is disqualified.
  • The rider must remain inside the coffin during the race or the team is disqualified.
  • No team-member substitutions after check-in.
  • All teams must follow event safety and competition directions.
