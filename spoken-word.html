WHAT IS SPOKEN WORD & RECITATION? ▾

Spoken word is poetry, storytelling, or other creative writing brought to life through a performer's voice, emotion, expression, and rhythm. It can be dramatic, funny, mysterious, or moving.

Recitation is the expressive reading or performance of a poem, story excerpt, or other literary passage—often a piece written by another author.

For Graveyard Stomp, think eerie poems, haunting tales, dramatic readings, and Gothic classics inspired by writers such as Edgar Allan Poe. You don't need elaborate costumes or stage props—just a story or poem to share and a voice to bring it to life.

All performances should be suitable for a family-friendly audience.

Perform With Us

Content must be appropriate for a family-friendly community event. This is a platform for artistic expression—not political, religious or personal preaching.
