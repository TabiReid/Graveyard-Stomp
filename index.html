GOOD TO KNOW BEFORE YOU GO

Frequently Asked Questions

When and where is the 2026 festival?

Friday, October 23 and Saturday, October 24, 2026, in Downtown Tonopah, Nevada. Individual activities have their own schedules.

How do I apply to be a vendor?

Complete the vendor application or download the printable agreement. Your space is not confirmed until the organizer approves participation.

How can I volunteer?

Complete the volunteer application. We'll follow up about assignments and scheduling.

How do I enter the Coffin Races or Pet Costume Contest?

Read the Coffin Races rules or the Pet Costume Contest details for entry information and contacts.

How can I donate or sponsor?

See the donation options, or email the organizer about sponsorship. Please confirm donation arrangements directly with the organizer.
